# Creative Production Standard (CPS) v1.0.0

## Purpose

CPS converts creative direction into a reviewable production system for brand, UI, image, video, and software deliverables. It separates creative preference from verifiable requirements and blocks release when evidence, rights, accessibility, or operational controls are incomplete.

## Creative ambition

CPS treats aesthetics as a designed point of view. Each release establishes a **signature tension**: two useful forces held in deliberate friction, such as archival precision × living motion or institutional calm × mischievous detail. This tension supplies a repeatable creative grammar without copying a reference work.

Every brief defines an **Aesthetic Field** with a signature tension, sensory materials, visual physics, information posture, human temperature, and a novelty hypothesis. The novelty hypothesis describes a combination of system decisions, never an imitation claim. “Unique” means original to this production record and materially distinct from supplied references; it does not claim global exclusivity.

## Contract map

| Contract | Governs | Release evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [Brand Policy](contracts/01-brand-policy.md) | identity, voice, messaging, visual direction | approved brief and claim ledger |
| [Accessible UI Requirements](contracts/02-accessible-ui.md) | interface behavior and WCAG 2.2 AA evidence | automated and manual accessibility report |
| [Generation Prompt Contract](contracts/03-generation-prompt-contract.md) | deterministic creative inputs and outputs | versioned prompt, source references, evaluation result |
| [Rights & Compliance Controls](contracts/04-rights-compliance.md) | IP, consent, privacy, marketing and regional obligations | rights register and approval record |
| [Production Operations Contract](contracts/05-production-operations.md) | execution, reproducibility, reliability, release | run record, artifact index, rollback plan |

## Required release packet

```text
creative_brief + brand_policy + claim_ledger + rights_register + generation_run_record
+ accessibility_report (when UI) + production_runbook + artifact_index + approvals
```

## Gate states

| State | Meaning | Allowed action |
|---|---|---|
| DRAFT | Inputs are being shaped. | Internal iteration |
| REVIEW | Required inputs exist; evidence is under review. | Private review only |
| APPROVED | Required owner approvals are recorded. | Produce release candidate |
| RELEASE_CANDIDATE | Validation passes and rollback exists. | Final human signoff |
| RELEASED | Signoff and immutable artifact index are complete. | Publish or deliver |
| BLOCKED | A required control lacks evidence or has failed. | Resolve named gap |

No asset may represent a customer, performance result, safety property, endorsement, certification, or legal condition without a matching entry in the claim ledger or rights register.

## Definition of done

1. The five relevant contracts have completed, evidenced fields.
2. Every public claim and supplied reference is traceable to a source or approval.
3. Generated assets retain source, model, prompt, version, and output-hash provenance.
4. UI deliverables meet the accessibility gate; video and audio deliverables include applicable captions, transcript, and reduced-motion treatment.
5. Rights, consent, privacy, and regional compliance have an owner and decision.
6. Release has a validated artifact index, a rollback path, and recorded signoff.
7. The Aesthetic Field, signature tension, and novelty hypothesis are visible in the final work and documented in the evaluation record.

## Signature review

Before approval, review the work at three distances:

| Distance | Question | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Three seconds | Does the atmosphere arrive before explanation? | First-impression reviewer notes |
| Thirty seconds | Does the hierarchy reveal a coherent point of view? | Composition and copy review |
| Three minutes | Does the system reward attention with usable detail and truthful substance? | Interaction, claim, and accessibility review |

Revisions name the failed distance and the changed design variable. This keeps feedback tied to a testable creative decision.
