# Contract 04 — Rights & Compliance Controls

Core constraint: distribution requires documented permission, provenance, privacy handling and applicable owner decisions. Unknown, restricted or expired rights block affected use unless the rights owner documents a valid permitted scope; an approval cannot create missing rights. Classify inputs before model submission. Preserve claim evidence and required disclosures. Do not submit confidential data to unapproved processors. Deliver a rights register and scoped review record; document creation is not compliance certification. Stop on unresolved rights/privacy concerns. External submission, takedown or publication requires authorization.

## Required output contract

Schema notation describes types, not completed evidence.

```yaml
rights_item:
  id: string
  asset_or_claim_ref: string
  type: logo|photo|video|music|font|copy|dataset|likeness|trademark|testimonial
  origin: owned|licensed|public_domain|user_supplied|generated
  license_or_consent_ref: string
  permitted_uses: [channel]
  territories: [country_or_region]
  term_end: date|null
  attribution_required: boolean
  restrictions: [string]
  approver: string
  status: clear|restricted|expired|unknown
```

Deliver rights register, claim references, data classification, decisions and incidents. Decisions bind hash, owner, evidence, market, permitted scope and date. `user_supplied`/`generated` never imply clearance.

## Controls

Verify assets/likenesses/testimonials/marks/music/fonts/references. Classify personal, sensitive, confidential and restricted data; record approved processors/locations, retention and deletion. Owners determine applicable market/audience/sector rules; financial/health/legal/employment/political claims trigger review, not invented universal mandates.

Evidence measurable/comparative/endorsement/environmental/security/availability/performance claims for the launch market. Preserve applicable disclosure placement, legibility, duration and locale. Disambiguate simulations; record likeness/voice provenance and material alterations.

Reference records retain contrast, palette temperature, density, pacing, materials, transformation, owner and similarity status; exclude copied logos/characters/signature layouts/marks/text. Review expression, trade dress, likeness and endorsement. No global originality guarantee. Carry input restrictions until reviewed as a conservative workflow rule, not a universal legal conclusion.

## Gate, incident and completion proof

Done: every item has a valid evidenced use decision; blocked items stay excluded. Incidents pause affected distribution/derivatives; preserve prompt/reference/artifact hashes, identify channels and notify owners through authorized means. Authorize takedown/correction separately; retain cause/resolution time. Resume after clearance. Evidence retrieval: three attempts maximum, then escalate.

## Edge cases

1. Expired license or mismatched territory: obtain valid scope or replace/remove the item.
2. Generated derivative with unclear inputs: retain unknown status and freeze affected release.
3. Conflicting legal interpretations: record uncertainty and route to the designated qualified owner.
