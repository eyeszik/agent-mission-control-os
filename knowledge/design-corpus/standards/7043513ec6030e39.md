# Contract 04 — Rights & Compliance Controls

## Objective

Establish permission, provenance, privacy, and approval evidence before distribution.

## Rights register

```yaml
rights_item:
  id: string
  asset_or_claim_ref: string
  type: logo|photo|video|music|font|copy|dataset|likeness|trademark|testimonial
  origin: owned|licensed|public_domain|user_supplied|generated
  license_or_consent_ref: string
  permitted_uses: [channel]
  territories: [country_or_region]
  term_end: date|null
  attribution_required: boolean
  restrictions: [string]
  approver: string
  status: clear|restricted|expired|unknown
```

## Controls

- Verify licenses and consent for source assets, likenesses, testimonials, customer marks, music, fonts, and reference materials.
- Classify inputs for personal data, sensitive personal data, confidential material, and client-restricted material before model submission.
- Use only approved processing locations, vendors, retention windows, and deletion paths for classified data.
- Route regulated, geographic, audience-specific, financial, health, legal, employment, or political claims to designated review owners.
- Maintain a claim ledger for measurable, comparative, endorsement, environmental, security, and availability claims.
- Record applicable requirements by market. Examples include privacy, advertising disclosures, accessibility, consumer protection, and sector rules; legal owners determine applicability.

## Reference abstraction record

Creative references may inform a direction without becoming a reproduction target. Store permitted observations separately from the source asset: contrast, palette temperature, density, pacing, material cues, intended transformation, review owner, and similarity-review status.

Exclude logos, characters, signature layouts, distinctive marks, and exact copy from the abstraction record. Reviewers compare released work with each high-influence reference for protected expression, trade dress, recognizable likeness, and misleading implied endorsement.

## Public-truth controls

For creative work that asks an audience to believe, buy, join, donate, register, or share:

- Link every measurable, comparative, environmental, performance, and testimonial claim to evidence current for the launch market.
- Preserve required disclosure proximity, legibility, duration, and locale with the same care used for primary copy.
- Distinguish conceptual visuals, simulated product screens, and documentary claims whenever a reasonable viewer could confuse them.
- Record human or synthetic likeness status, voice status, and material alteration status when the asset presents a real person or realistic event.

## Gate

`clear` status is required for every distributed asset and claim. `unknown`, `expired`, and `restricted` require removal or written owner approval defining the exact permitted exception. Generated output inherits the most restrictive applicable input restriction until a reviewer clears it.

## Incident response

For rights, privacy, or public-claim issues: halt distribution, identify affected artifact hashes and channels, preserve evidence, notify the assigned owner, execute takedown/correction, and record resolution time and root cause.

For a similarity concern, freeze derivatives, retain prompts and reference hashes, scope affected campaign surfaces, and route the record to the assigned rights owner. Resume only after the revised output and decision record are cleared.
