## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Source direction: audit-style KPI hierarchy with prior-period comparison ticks.

## 2. Color
Observed: Canvas #FAFAFB — viewport background. Never use pure white as the canvas; the warm off-white separates cards from the page. Surface #FFFFFF — every card, KPI tile, table, and the right-side ranking list. Surface-muted #F4F5F7 — the empty track behind crimson mini-bars in the lowest-OTD list, and the subtle hover state on table rows.

## 3. Typography
Observed: Page title (On-Time Delivery Dashboard) — 17px, weight 700, tight tracking. No subtitle, no decorative emoji. Card title (OTD % (LINES) BY KEY ACCOUNT, LOWEST OTD % ACCOUNTS (≥10 LINES)) — 12.5px, weight 700, uppercase, +0.04em tracking. Always uppercase. This is the editorial signature. KPI label (OTD % (LINES), YOY Δ (PTS)) — 10.5px, weight 600, uppercase, +0.06em tracking, in text-secondary.

## 4. Spacing & Grid
Observed: Spacing scale: 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 28px. Do not invent intermediate values. Card padding: 14–18px (KPI tiles 14px vertical, 16px horizontal; bar chart card 16/18px). Vertical gap between page header and the KPI strip: 12px.

## 5. Layout & Composition
Observed: 1. Header row (full width, 36px tall): page title on the far left + filter-pill cluster on the far right. No back-button, no breadcrumb, no avatar. Right, 3/8 width: "LOWEST OTD % ACCOUNTS (≥10 LINES)" — ranked table with red mini-bars and a tabular OTD-LINES count column. The grid is rigid. Do not introduce a sidebar, a topbar, or a hero strip; this style is meant for a single full-bleed dashboard panel that can be embedded in a report page.

## 6. Components
Observed: Left slot (label, ~150px, right-aligned): account name in 12.5px text-secondary. Middle slot (track + fill, fluid): a 12px-tall data-track track filling the slot, with a data-ink fill from 0% to the current-period percentage. Always overlaid by a 2px-wide, 16px-tall vertical crimson tick at the prior-year percentage position. The tick extends 2px above and 2px below the bar so it visually punctures the track.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Observed: No hover lift on cards. No row hover fill on tables (a barely visible surface-muted background is permitted, but discouraged). Tooltip on bar hover (optional): a small 4px-radius white card with a 1px border, listing exact Current and Prior year percentages and the LINES count. Never animate its appearance with anything heavier than opacity 120ms ease-out.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Observed: Never use exclamation marks. Never use emoji. Never use first-person ("our", "your", "we").

## 9. Anti-patterns
Adapt source restrictions to the task; accessibility, semantic state, and authorized identity outrank style prescriptions.
