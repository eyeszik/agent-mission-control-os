## Core constraint
Translate the observed aesthetic into an original, usable design; do not copy brand assets, identities or proprietary fonts.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Light conversation surface; deep sidebar anchor; unread weight and optional thread pane.

## Color
Observed samples: `#4A154B`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F8F8F8`, `#350d36`, `#611f69`. Treat as evidence, not approved tokens; choose semantic roles and measure actual foreground/background pairs.

## Typography
Source inventory: | Display / Marketing Headings | Larsseit | 'Larsseit', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif |. Verify licensing, glyph coverage and real weight availability; preserve hierarchy with approved fallbacks.

## Layout & Components
Maintain channel/context/thread hierarchy without reproducing the platform identity. Translate source spacing into content-driven responsive layouts; define focus, loading, empty and error states.

## Motion & Interaction
Use motion only for meaningful feedback; retain a static/reduced-motion alternative and keyboard access.

## Edge cases and context gaps
Three panes crowd narrow screens; unread status needs more than color. Missing: target users, content, viewport range, assets and implementation constraints. Ask only blocking questions; otherwise label assumptions.
