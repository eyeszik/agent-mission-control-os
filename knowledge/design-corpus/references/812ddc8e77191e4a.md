## 6. Depth & Elevation
Observed: Text (Level 2): treatment Spectral white text, no shadow, use Content layer — text floats directly on image. *Shadow Philosophy: SpaceX uses ZERO shadows. In a design built entirely on photography, shadows are meaningless — every surface is already a photograph with natural lighting. Depth comes from the photographic content itself: the receding curvature of Earth, the diminishing trail of a rocket, the atmospheric haze around Mars.
