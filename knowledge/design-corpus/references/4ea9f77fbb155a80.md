## 6. Components
Observed: No drop shadow. Elevation is communicated through border contrast, not shadow. Tabs use --text-secondary by default. Active tab: --text-primary + 2px bottom border in --accent. No background highlight on the active tab.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Observed: Hover state change: duration 120ms, easing ease. Panel / sidebar open: duration 160ms, easing ease-out.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Observed: Precise: Say the thing directly. No throat-clearing ("Great question!"), no filler phrases. Cited: Every factual claim is attributed. Hedging language ("according to", "reportedly") is preferred over assertions.

## 9. Anti-patterns
