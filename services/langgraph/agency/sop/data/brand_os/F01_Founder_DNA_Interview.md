## Purpose
Elicit the founder’s evidence-backed motivations, formative experiences, operating principles, ambitions, constraints, and non-negotiables that may inform a brand. Separate remembered facts from present interpretations and desired public positioning.

## Inputs and preparation
- Founder/decision-maker identity and consent to record or take notes.
- Current business, product, audience, stage, and reason for the interview.
- Known brand materials, if supplied; do not infer missing history.
- Interviewer, note-taker, date, duration, and approved retention location.

Send a short pre-interview note explaining purpose, expected duration (45–60 minutes), intended use, who will see notes, whether recording is optional, and how the participant can correct or withdraw sensitive material.

## Procedure
1. **Set boundaries.** Confirm consent, recording choice, confidentiality limits, and topics the founder does not want included. Do not solicit credentials, protected personal data, or confidential third-party information.
2. **Establish chronology.** Ask about origin, pivotal decisions, setbacks, customer encounters, and changes in direction. Use open questions; avoid leading language.
3. **Elicit principles through examples.** For each stated value, ask for a specific decision or behavior that demonstrates it, an exception, and a cost the founder accepted.
4. **Explore motivation and ambition.** Ask what problem feels worth solving, what success means over defined time horizons, and what the organization refuses to trade away.
5. **Test distinctiveness.** Ask which beliefs or methods are uncommon in the category and what observable evidence supports that claim. Record competing explanations.
6. **Map constraints.** Capture capabilities, resources, legal/ethical boundaries, current commitments, and areas where the founder is uncertain.
7. **Reflect back.** Summarize themes and ask the founder to correct, qualify, or reject them. Do not turn a hypothesis into a quote or fact.
8. **Synthesize.** Produce a concise founder profile with direct quotations clearly marked, paraphrases labeled, evidence references, confidence, unresolved tensions, and questions for follow-up.

## Output
A dated interview record; founder narrative; 3–7 candidate principles, each linked to a concrete example; ambition and constraint statements; confirmed quotations; interpretation/evidence distinction; unresolved questions; consent and retention note.

## Quality and stop conditions
Pass when every proposed principle has an example or is explicitly marked unverified, the founder has reviewed the synthesis, and sensitive details are excluded or explicitly approved. Stop and ask for clarification where accounts conflict, consent is absent, or a claim could expose confidential information. The output is an input to later brand work, not an approved brand platform.
