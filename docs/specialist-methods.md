Recommended methods (activate only for an applicable work item)

2. Creative continuity. For recurring IP freeze approved series, character, world, brand, visual and production references. Separate invariant from permitted variation. Deliver a canon delta and revision brief to the existing Project OS artifact/review path after checking its schema. The reviewer must locate a seeded continuity error and distinguish a legitimate evolution. If canon, actual media or rights are missing, qualify the review and hold dependent release.
3. Commercial productization. For a proposed owned or client product bind beneficiary, rights, catalog identity, version, variant, channel and fulfillment owner. Inspect actual package bytes and channel rules. Tie dated market claims to sources and buyer evidence. Show net unit revenue, variable cost, contribution and fixed costs separately with units and assumptions. Produce one canonical manifest and channel-specific previews. Fail on mismatched bytes, unauthorized rights or unsupported claims. Use existing project/work-order and approval paths. No listings, client messaging or spend.
4. Independent acceptance. On a candidate artifact or material revision, pin its digest, controlling brief and test fixture. Reopen the output in the intended medium. Separate objective checks from judgment. Blind the reviewer to origin where feasible. Record evidence pointer, severity, disposition and exact reviewed version. A critical rights, security, functionality or technical failure holds release. Changed bytes invalidate the old approval. A generator reviewing its own result cannot sign independent acceptance.
