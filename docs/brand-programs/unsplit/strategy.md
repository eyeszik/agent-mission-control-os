## 2. Brand strategy

### 2.1 Category

The category is not "route posters". That category sells a **place**: a marathon
course, a favourite climb, a city. It is crowded, price-anchored at $39–$79, and
dominated by map tiles (S6, S7).

UNSPLIT creates an adjacent category: the **running year, as an object**. It
sells **time and accumulated effort**, not geography. No maps, no tiles, no
streets. One continuous line, plotted from a year of traces.

### 2.2 Audience insight

Runners already keep the record. Strava holds 200M+ users (S2) and records
billions of activities; the Year in Sport recap is free, digital, and identical
for everyone. What is missing is not data but **permanence**: a way to own the
year rather than scroll past it.

The insight, stated as a proposal: *the miles that matter most are the ones
nobody saw.* The easy days, the dark mornings, the runs that never became a post.
A highlight reel cannot hold them. A line can.

### 2.3 Positioning

> For runners who already log every mile, **UNSPLIT** is the atelier that turns a
> year of movement into one continuous, numbered line — a record, not a
> highlight reel. Unlike route posters that sell a place, UNSPLIT sells time:
> 365 days of effort, drawn once, never repeated.

### 2.4 Brand idea

**One year. One line.**

The line is literal (one polyline), structural (one continuous effort) and
verbal (one sentence you can repeat). It also carries the name: *unsplit* means
the whole year, not the split times.

### 2.5 Pillars

1. **Whole, not highlights.** The easy runs are in the line. No filtering for
   the impressive days.
2. **Machine-made, hand-finished.** Automation draws it; a human-numbered,
   stamped certificate finishes it. The machine is disclosed, not hidden.
3. **The line is the evidence.** Every print carries its own data summary and a
   short content hash. No decorative statistics.
4. **Quiet pride.** Understatement as a flex. The object does the talking.

### 2.6 Voice

Precise, warm, dry. Short sentences. Real numbers. No exclamation marks. Never
"crush", "grind", "beast", "journey", "unlock", "level up".

| Context | Write this | Not this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | One year. One line. | CRUSH YOUR GOALS 💪 |
| Product | The Annual. 50 × 70 cm. Edition 1 of 1. | Premium limited-edition masterpiece |
| Caption | You didn't post the easy days. They're in the line. | Every run tells a story ✨ |
| Certificate | 2,418.6 km · 187 runs · 31,204 m climbed · 01.01–31.12 | #RunningGoals #Strava |

### 2.7 Naming and offer architecture

| Name | What it is | Proposed price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Annual** | 50 × 70 cm archival print, edition 1/1, numbered certificate | USD 120 / GBP 95 / EUR 110 |
| **The Half** | A3 print of a six-month season | USD 65 / GBP 52 / EUR 60 |
| **The Crew Sheet** | A club's composite season: one sheet, every member's line | From USD 450 (master) + member prints at 15% off |
| **The Digital Line** | SVG + PDF, self-print | USD 25 |

Naming is **uncleared**. No trademark search, domain check, or company-registry
search has been performed. "UNSPLIT" must be cleared before any public use.

### 2.8 The automated mechanism

```
runner authorises Strava / uploads GPX-FIT
        ↓
normalise activities → cumulative distance axis → pace/elevation envelope
        ↓
one polyline + tick register + data legend + certificate hash
        ↓
contrast / legibility / bounds / text-safety checks  (fail = no render)
        ↓
print-ready PDF/SVG → regional POD partner → shipped
        ↓
Project OS artifact (version, lineage, proof) + provenance manifest
```

Human labour per order: zero. Human labour per **edition**: numbering and a
physical check, which is the premium signal and also the quality gate. That is
the whole business: automation makes a one-off economically sane; the
hand-finished certificate makes it worth $120 rather than $39.

## 3. Three creative directions

All three use the same product truth and differ in **what the line is allowed to
feel like**. Contrast figures below were measured with
`agency.ui_ux.tokens.wcag_contrast_ratio` against each direction's paper colour.
All three pass AA for body text and AAA for ink; in every direction, text is
never set in an accent colour.

### Direction A — REGISTER

**Concept.** The print is a readout from an instrument that has been running for
a year. Automation is not hidden; it is the aesthetic. Calibration ticks, a
registration mark, a mono data legend, and one continuous signal. The line is
the only expressive thing on the sheet, so it carries all the emotion by
contrast.

**Visual system**

| Token | Value | Measured contrast on paper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| paper | `#0E0F0C` | — |
| ink | `#F2F0EA` | 16.87:1 |
| accent | `#D7FF3E` (signal lime) | 16.72:1 |
| accent_2 | `#7A8CFF` (plotter blue) | 6.43:1 |

Note: ink on accent is **1.01:1**, so text never sits on lime. Lime is a line,
a tick, or a 2 px rule only.

- **Type:** Inter Display 700, −0.02em tracking, sentence case for headlines;
  JetBrains Mono for every number, label and certificate field; micro-labels at
  11 px, 0.12em tracking, uppercase.
- **Mark:** a hairline baseline that runs past the wordmark and breaks once. In
  the foundry this is expressed with `stacked_bars` plus rules rather than a
  custom glyph.
- **Grid:** 12 columns, 8 px baseline, 8% margin. Everything aligns to the tick
  register.
- **Texture:** flat, no grain. One-pixel rules and a visible 10 mm calibration
  strip at the foot of the sheet.
- **Motion:** the line draws once, left to right, 2.5 s, ease-out, then holds.
  Reduced motion: final frame only.
- **Grammar weights:** `swiss_editorial` 0.55, `retro_computing` 0.30,
  `modern_minimalism` 0.15.

**Verbal**

> One year. One line.
> Every run you logged, including the ones you didn't post.
> 2,418.6 km. 187 runs. One line. Edition 1 of 1.

**Applications it suits.** Poster, certificate, packaging band, website hero,
app icon (a single tick on black). Strongest for the data-literate buyer and for
making the automation legible as craft.

**Risks.** Lime is a trend colour and will date; black stock and light ink raise
print cost and are harder to proof; the instrument voice can read cold and
male-coded. **Kill evidence:** buyers describe the object as "a graph" or "a
dashboard", not as "a record".

### Direction B — NIGHT SHIFT

**Concept.** The private half of a running year. The line is the route you saw
from a car window at 9 pm: sodium light, wet asphalt, no audience. Cinematic and
social-first, built for the year-end feed without becoming a motivational post.

**Visual system**

| Token | Value | Measured contrast on paper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| paper | `#0B1020` | — |
| ink | `#F4F1E8` | 16.76:1 |
| accent | `#FF6B35` (sodium) | 6.68:1 |
| accent_2 | `#9BB7FF` (cold headlight) | 9.57:1 |

- **Type:** Inter Display 600, sentence case, wide tracking (0.06em) for
  headlines; mono only for the certificate block. Softer than A, still exact.
- **Mark:** a long ember line with one terminal dot, like a cigarette end or a
  rear light. Wordmark lowercase `unsplit` at 0.2em tracking.
- **Grid:** 6 columns, 12 px baseline, asymmetric lower-left anchor.
- **Texture:** fine grain and halation on the line only; no glow on type.
- **Motion:** the line ignites from the left and settles, 2.8 s, hold. Reduced
  motion: the lit final frame.
- **Grammar weights:** `cinematic_typography` 0.45, `modern_minimalism` 0.35,
  `dimensional_glass` 0.20.

**Verbal**

> The city sleeps. You don't.
> Your best miles were never on the feed.
> Run at 5:41 am. Nobody clapped. It still counts.

**Applications it suits.** Social campaign, year-end film, gift cards, the
website's opening frame. Strongest for acquisition and for the 25–34 urban
runner.

**Risks.** Reads as nightlife or a headphone brand; glow is a screen effect that
does not survive matte paper without spot varnish; social-first can undercut a
$120 object. **Kill evidence:** unassisted recall is "a running app" or "a
poster brand", not "a printed record".

### Direction C — FIELD RECORD

**Concept.** A naturalist's specimen sheet for a year of movement. The print is
filed, not posted: stamped, numbered, dated, annotated. It treats effort as
field data and the runner as the observer.

**Visual system**

| Token | Value | Measured contrast on paper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| paper | `#EFE7D6` | — |
| ink | `#1B1A17` | 14.15:1 |
| accent | `#9E2F23` (oxide) | 5.92:1 |
| accent_2 | `#44584A` (field green) | 6.23:1 |

- **Type:** Inter Display 600 with generous leading, sentence case; mono for
  the field block; letterspaced caps for the stamp only.
- **Mark:** a pressed-line mark inside a hairline box — a line that has been
  flattened between two pages. Wordmark in caps, 0.16em, with a rule above.
- **Grid:** 8 columns, 8 px baseline, 9% margin, wide bottom field block.
- **Texture:** risograph grain, slight plate offset, rubber-stamp overprint,
  hand-written edition number.
- **Motion:** none by default; a 0.6 s fade of the field block on web. Reduced
  motion: no transition.
- **Grammar weights:** `tactile_paper` 0.45, `swiss_editorial` 0.35,
  `modern_minimalism` 0.20.

**Verbal**

> A record of the year you kept moving.
> Filed under: weather, early mornings, unfinished conversations.
> Season 2026. 187 entries. No omissions.

**Applications it suits.** Gift packaging, gallery/retail, club archives,
printed certificate. Strongest for gifting and for a higher price tier.

**Risks.** Nostalgia can tip into memorial, which is the wrong emotion for a
living runner; grain and stamp effects are hard to reproduce consistently in
POD; the palette is less scroll-stopping. **Kill evidence:** recipients read the
object as a memorial or a vintage pastiche.

---

## 4. Recommendation

**Recommend Direction A — REGISTER**, with two borrowings: B's ignition motion
for the web hero and year-end film, and C's stamped, hand-numbered certificate
inside the tube. The reason is strategic, not aesthetic: REGISTER makes the
automation itself the visible craft, which is the only defensible difference
from a $39 map poster. B is the best acquisition layer; C is the best gift layer.

This is a recommendation. It is not a decision, and it is not approved.
